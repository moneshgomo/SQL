1. Surrogate key 
    - Unique for each version of the customer record 
    - example : customer_key with AUTO INCREMENT
    - Artificial Unique key 

2. Natural key 
    - Unique identifier for the customer ( columns are always Unique)
    - example : Aadhaar card number 
    - It can be a PRIMARY KEY ( must in all tables )
